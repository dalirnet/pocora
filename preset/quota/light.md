# Light

For a child who uses the phone for chat, school and simple games, or a family on a small data package.

- **Per mark (30 minutes):** 25 MB
- **Per hour:** 50 MB

## Enough for

- Chat and messages, with photos
- School apps, messages and files
- Browsing
- Simple online games

## Not enough for

- Video, beyond a few minutes
- Video calls
- Live online classes
- Social apps with a video feed

## Most per month

The most a child can use in a 30-day month, if every Allowed mark is used to its quota. Real use is lower.
