# Very high

For online school all day, or a family with a large data package.

- **Per mark (30 minutes):** 500 MB
- **Per hour:** 1000 MB

## Enough for

- Everything in High
- High-quality video
- Long video calls and live classes

## Not enough for

- Several devices sharing one connection

## Most per month

The most a child can use in a 30-day month, if every Allowed mark is used to its quota. Real use is lower.
