# High

For a child who watches video or has live online classes, on a package that can carry it.

- **Per mark (30 minutes):** 250 MB
- **Per hour:** 500 MB

## Enough for

- Everything in Medium
- Video at normal quality for the whole mark
- Video calls
- Live online classes

## Not enough for

- High-quality video for the whole mark
- Large downloads such as game updates

## Most per month

The most a child can use in a 30-day month, if every Allowed mark is used to its quota. Real use is lower.
