# Minimal

For a young child, or a family on a very small data package. Messages and school only.

- **Per mark (30 minutes):** 10 MB
- **Per hour:** 20 MB

## Enough for

- Chat and messages, without much video
- School apps, messages and files

## Not enough for

- Browsing, beyond a little
- Online games
- Music
- Any video

## Most per month

The most a child can use in a 30-day month, if every Allowed mark is used to its quota. Real use is lower.
