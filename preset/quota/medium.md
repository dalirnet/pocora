# Medium

For most children. Everyday use without long video.

- **Per mark (30 minutes):** 100 MB
- **Per hour:** 200 MB

## Enough for

- Everything in Light
- Music
- Online games
- Social apps
- Short or low-quality video

## Not enough for

- Video for the whole mark
- Live online classes with video
- Long video calls

## Most per month

The most a child can use in a 30-day month, if every Allowed mark is used to its quota. Real use is lower.
