# Fairly high

For a teenager who watches some video, or a child in online classes part of the day.

- **Per mark (30 minutes):** 150 MB
- **Per hour:** 300 MB

## Enough for

- Everything in Medium
- Normal-quality video for part of the mark
- Video calls and live classes

## Not enough for

- High-quality video for the whole mark

## Most per month

The most a child can use in a 30-day month, if every Allowed mark is used to its quota. Real use is lower.
