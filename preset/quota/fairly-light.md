# Fairly light

For a child who chats, browses and plays, with a little video now and then.

- **Per mark (30 minutes):** 50 MB
- **Per hour:** 100 MB

## Enough for

- Everything in Light
- Music
- Online games
- A few short videos

## Not enough for

- Video for the whole mark
- Video calls
- Live online classes

## Most per month

The most a child can use in a 30-day month, if every Allowed mark is used to its quota. Real use is lower.
