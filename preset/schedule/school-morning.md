# School, morning shift

For a child who is at school in the morning, Saturday to Wednesday. The default choice for most families from Mehr to Khordad.

- **Allowed per week:** 30h 30m
- **When to use:** The normal school year.

## Season

| From | To | Month-day |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mehr 1 | Azar 30 | 07-01 to 09-30 |
| Dey 21 | Esfand 24 | 10-21 to 12-24 |
| Farvardin 14 | Ordibehesht 31 | 01-14 to 02-31 |

Suggested first in these periods, together with **School, afternoon shift**. The parent picks the one that matches the child's shift.

## Allowed blocks

| Days | Allowed |
| --- | --- |
| Sat to Wed | 17:00 to 20:30 |
| Thu | 10:00 to 12:00, 17:00 to 22:00 |
| Fri | 10:00 to 12:00, 16:00 to 20:00 |

Every other hour is Limited.

## Why these hours

- School runs from about 07:30 to 13:00, so the morning is Limited. There is nothing to check before school.
- 13:00 to 17:00 is for lunch, rest and homework. Internet opens only after homework time.
- Allowed time ends at 20:30, leaving dinner and a quiet hour before sleep on a school night.
- Thursday is the free day: a morning block, a long break for the family lunch, and the longest evening of the week.
- Friday evening ends at 20:00, because Saturday is a school day.

## Adjust

- School on Thursday: clear the Thursday morning block.
- Rotating shift (one week morning, one week afternoon): switch between this preset and **School, afternoon shift** each week.
- Evening class (language, sport): clear those slots so the block starts when the child is home.
