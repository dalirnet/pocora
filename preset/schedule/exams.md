# Exam season

For the exam weeks of Dey and Khordad, and for a child preparing for an entrance exam.

- **Allowed per week:** 13h 30m
- **When to use:** Two to three weeks around term exams.

## Season

| From | To | Month-day |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dey 1 | Dey 20 | 10-01 to 10-20 |
| Khordad 1 | Khordad 25 | 03-01 to 03-25 |

Suggested first in these periods. Term exams apply mostly from grade 7 up. Primary school has no formal exams, so for a younger child this stays a suggestion the parent can skip.

## Allowed blocks

| Days | Allowed |
| --- | --- |
| Sat to Wed | 13:00 to 13:30, 20:00 to 21:00 |
| Thu and Fri | 13:00 to 14:00, 19:00 to 21:00 |

Every other hour is Limited.

## Why these hours

- Exams are in the morning and the rest of the day is for studying the next subject.
- A half-hour break after lunch is a real rest and is short enough not to swallow the afternoon.
- One hour in the evening, 20:00 to 21:00, is something to look forward to after a day of study, and ends well before sleep.
- The half-hour after lunch can use the **Study** apps preset, leaving the evening hour for free use.
- Thursday and Friday are still study days in exam season, so they get three hours, not a full weekend.

## Adjust

- After the last exam, switch back to the school preset, or to **Summer** in Khordad.
- If the child studies with online material, add an Allowed block for it rather than opening the whole day.
