# Holidays

For Nowruz, long weekends and family trips, when normal rules would only cause arguments.

- **Allowed per week:** 91h
- **When to use:** Nowruz and other holiday stretches of a few days to two weeks.

## Season

| From | To | Month-day |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Esfand 25 | Farvardin 13 | 12-25 to 01-13 |

Suggested first for Nowruz. For a single public holiday, use a custom block instead of switching.

## Allowed blocks

| Days | Allowed |
| --- | --- |
| Every day | 10:00 to 23:00 |

Every other hour is Limited.

## Why these hours

- Holidays are irregular: visits, guests, travel, late nights. A detailed schedule will not match the day.
- One long block keeps only two rules: no phone first thing in the morning, and no internet after 23:00.
- Everything in between is left to the family in the room.

## Adjust

- During visits, add a custom Limited block for those hours.
- When school starts again, switch back to the school preset the evening before.
