# Ramadan

For school weeks during Ramadan, when the family's day moves around iftar.

- **Allowed per week:** 33h 30m
- **When to use:** The month of Ramadan, while school is open.

## Season

Follows the lunar calendar: the 9th Hijri month, day 1 to the last day. It moves about 11 days earlier each year, so the app takes its dates from [`holidays/`](../holidays/README.md). Suggested only when Ramadan falls inside a school period. **Exam season** and **Holidays** come before it when they overlap.

## Allowed blocks

| Days | Allowed |
| --- | --- |
| Sat to Wed | 15:00 to 17:00, 19:30 to 21:30 |
| Thu | 11:00 to 13:00, 15:00 to 17:00, 19:30 to 23:00 |
| Fri | 11:00 to 13:00, 15:00 to 17:00, 19:30 to 21:30 |

Every other hour is Limited.

## Why these hours

- School days are shorter in Ramadan, so the afternoon block starts earlier, at 15:00.
- 17:00 to 19:30 is Limited: the hours before iftar and the iftar table belong to the family.
- The evening block runs later than usual, to 21:30, because the household is awake later.
- Thursday night runs to 23:00. Friday night ends at 21:30, because Saturday is a school day.

## Adjust

- Iftar moves with the season. Shift the Limited gap so it ends about an hour after sunset where you live.
- A child who wakes for sahari needs sleep earlier: end the evening block at 21:00.
