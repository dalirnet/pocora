# Summer

For the three months without school, Tir to Shahrivar.

- **Allowed per week:** 46h
- **When to use:** Summer holiday.

## Season

| From | To | Month-day |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Khordad 26 | Shahrivar 31 | 03-26 to 06-31 |

Suggested first in this period, starting when final exams end.

## Allowed blocks

| Days | Allowed |
| --- | --- |
| Sat to Wed | 10:30 to 12:30, 18:00 to 22:00 |
| Thu and Fri | 10:30 to 12:30, 17:00 to 23:00 |

Every other hour is Limited.

## Why these hours

- Without a schedule, summer becomes all-day phone use. This preset gives two clear windows instead.
- A late-morning block, after breakfast and some time awake without a screen.
- 12:30 to 18:00 is Limited: lunch, the hot hours, rest, books, offline games and summer classes.
- A long evening block to 22:00, when the family is up late and the day has cooled.
- Thursday and Friday start an hour earlier in the evening and run to 23:00.

## Adjust

- Summer class in the morning: move the first block to after the class.
- If the long midday gap is too hard, add one hour at 15:00 rather than joining the two blocks.
- On a trip, switch to **Holidays**.
