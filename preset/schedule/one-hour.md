# One hour a day

For younger children, about 6 to 10, who are just starting with their own phone or tablet.

- **Allowed per week:** 9h
- **When to use:** All year, for a child who should have a small fixed amount.

## Season

Any time of year. Never suggested by date. Always listed for the parent to choose.

## Allowed blocks

| Days | Allowed |
| --- | --- |
| Sat to Wed | 18:00 to 19:00 |
| Thu and Fri | 11:00 to 12:00, 18:00 to 19:00 |

Every other hour is Limited.

## Why these hours

- A small fixed hour at the same time every day is easy for a young child to understand and accept.
- 18:00 is after homework and before dinner.
- Thursday and Friday add a second hour before lunch.
- Offline apps still work the rest of the day. Only the internet is held back.

## Adjust

- Move the hour to whenever a parent is normally at home.
- As the child grows, widen the block, or move to **School, morning shift**.
