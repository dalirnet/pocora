# Weekends only

For families whose rule is: no internet on school days.

- **Allowed per week:** 10h
- **When to use:** All year, often for younger children or after a period of heavy use.

## Season

Any time of year. Never suggested by date. Most useful in the school periods.

## Allowed blocks

| Days | Allowed |
| --- | --- |
| Sat to Wed | None |
| Thu | 16:00 to 21:00 |
| Fri | 10:00 to 12:00, 16:00 to 19:00 |

Every other hour is Limited.

## Why these hours

- School days are fully Limited. The rule is simple and there is nothing to negotiate each evening.
- Thursday afternoon and evening is the main block of the week.
- Friday has a morning block and an afternoon block that ends at 19:00, ahead of the school week.

## Adjust

- If the school sends homework through Shad, add a short weekday block for it.
- School on Thursday: start the Thursday block later.
