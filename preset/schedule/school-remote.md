# Online school

For weeks when school is closed and classes move online: air pollution, cold, heat or power shortages.

- **Allowed per week:** 55h 30m
- **When to use:** School is remote for several days or more.

## Season

Not tied to a date, since closures are announced a day or two ahead. Offered as an alternative during the school periods, and shown higher from Aban to Bahman (08-01 to 11-30), when pollution and cold closures are most common.

## Allowed blocks

| Days | Allowed |
| --- | --- |
| Sat to Wed | 07:30 to 12:30, 17:00 to 20:30 |
| Thu | 10:00 to 12:00, 17:00 to 22:00 |
| Fri | 10:00 to 12:00, 16:00 to 20:00 |

Every other hour is Limited.

## Why these hours

- Class happens on the phone, in Shad or the school's own tool, so the morning must be Allowed from 07:30 to 12:30.
- The morning block uses the **School only** apps preset, so class works and games and video do not.
- 12:30 to 17:00 stays Limited, exactly as on a normal school day: lunch, rest, homework.
- The evening block and the weekend are the same as **School, morning shift**, so the child's free time does not grow just because school is closed.

## Adjust

- A single closed day: do not switch presets. Add a custom Allowed block for that morning.
- Afternoon online classes: move the first block to the class hours.
