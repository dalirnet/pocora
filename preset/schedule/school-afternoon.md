# School, afternoon shift

For a child in a two-shift school who attends in the afternoon, roughly 12:30 to 17:30.

- **Allowed per week:** 28h
- **When to use:** Afternoon-shift weeks of the school year.

## Season

| From | To | Month-day |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mehr 1 | Azar 30 | 07-01 to 09-30 |
| Dey 21 | Esfand 24 | 10-21 to 12-24 |
| Farvardin 14 | Ordibehesht 31 | 01-14 to 02-31 |

Suggested in these periods as the alternative to **School, morning shift**.

## Allowed blocks

| Days | Allowed |
| --- | --- |
| Sat to Wed | 09:30 to 11:00, 19:00 to 20:30 |
| Thu | 10:00 to 12:00, 17:00 to 22:00 |
| Fri | 10:00 to 12:00, 16:00 to 20:00 |

Every other hour is Limited.

## Why these hours

- The morning is this child's homework time. Internet opens at 09:30, after the first study hour, and closes at 11:00 so there is time for lunch and getting ready.
- 11:00 to 19:00 covers lunch, the way to school, school itself and the way home.
- A shorter evening block, 19:00 to 20:30, since the child comes home late and is tired.
- Thursday and Friday are the same as the morning-shift preset.

## Adjust

- Rotating shift: switch between this preset and **School, morning shift** each week.
- If homework is done in the evening instead, move the morning block later and shorten the evening one.
