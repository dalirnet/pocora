# Watch only

For families who want to see, not to limit.

- **Allowed per week:** 168h
- **When to use:** The first week with Pocora, or with a child who has earned full trust.

## Season

Any time of year. Suggested once, for the first week after a child's phone is paired.

## Allowed blocks

| Days | Allowed |
| --- | --- |
| Every day | 00:00 to 24:00 |

Every other hour is Limited.

## Why these hours

- Every hour is Allowed. Pocora holds nothing back.
- App usage, screen time and events are still recorded and shown to the parent.
- A good first week: look at the real pattern before choosing a preset.

## Adjust

- After a week, pick the preset closest to what you saw and adjust from there.
