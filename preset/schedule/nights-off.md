# Nights off

For older children, about 13 to 16, who manage their own day but should not be online at night.

- **Allowed per week:** 106h 30m
- **When to use:** All year, when the only concern is sleep.

## Season

Any time of year. Never suggested by date. Always listed for the parent to choose.

## Allowed blocks

| Days | Allowed |
| --- | --- |
| Sat to Wed, and Fri | 07:00 to 22:00 |
| Thu | 07:00 to 23:30 |

Every other hour is Limited.

## Why these hours

- The whole day is Allowed. This preset protects one thing: sleep.
- Internet closes at 22:00 on nights before a school day, which includes Friday night.
- Thursday night runs to 23:30.
- It opens at 07:00, so there is no reason to wake early for the phone.

## Adjust

- No school on Thursday: extend Wednesday night to 23:30 as well.
- Add a custom Limited block for school hours if daytime use becomes a problem.
