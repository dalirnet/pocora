# Everyday

For a child who uses the phone for school, family chat, games and video. The default for most children.

- **Age:** 10 to 12
- **Groups with internet:** 10 of 13
- **Goes well with:** **School, morning shift**, **School, afternoon shift**, **Summer**

## Apps

```
[x] System       Phone, messages, keyboard, updates
[x] School       Shad, live class apps
[x] Learning     Schoolwork, languages, books, Quran
[x] Kids         Digitoon, Aparat Kids, stories
[x] Messaging    WhatsApp, Eitaa, Bale, iGap
[x] Daily tools  Maps, taxi, calendar, weather
[x] Music        Music, radio, podcasts
[x] Video        Telewebion, Aparat, Filimo
[x] Games        Every game
[x] Stores       Bazaar, Myket, Google Play
[ ] Browser      Chrome, Firefox and others
[ ] Social       Rubika, Chatzy, Wisgoon
[ ] Other        Shopping, banking, anything unlisted
```

`[x]` has internet in an Allowed mark. `[ ]` does not. The apps in each group are in [`groups/`](./groups/README.md).

## Why this list

- Everything a child this age normally uses, without the two open doors: the browser and social networks.
- Messaging is in, so the child can talk to family and classmates.
- Games, video and music are in. The schedule and the quota decide how much.
- Stores are in, so apps update. A newly installed app still lands in its own group.

## Adjust

- Homework needs the web: mark the browser as always in.
- Move to **Teen** around age 13.
