# Teen

For a teenager who needs the web for school and daily life, in a family that holds back social networks.

- **Age:** 13 to 16
- **Groups with internet:** 12 of 13
- **Goes well with:** **School, morning shift**, **Nights off**

## Apps

```
[x] System       Phone, messages, keyboard, updates
[x] School       Shad, live class apps
[x] Learning     Schoolwork, languages, books, Quran
[x] Kids         Digitoon, Aparat Kids, stories
[x] Messaging    WhatsApp, Eitaa, Bale, iGap
[x] Daily tools  Maps, taxi, calendar, weather
[x] Music        Music, radio, podcasts
[x] Video        Telewebion, Aparat, Filimo
[x] Games        Every game
[x] Stores       Bazaar, Myket, Google Play
[x] Browser      Chrome, Firefox and others
[ ] Social       Rubika, Chatzy, Wisgoon
[x] Other        Shopping, banking, anything unlisted
```

`[x]` has internet in an Allowed mark. `[ ]` does not. The apps in each group are in [`groups/`](./groups/README.md).

## Why this list

- Adds the browser and everything unlisted: shopping, banking, tools.
- Social networks are the only group left out. For many families that is the one remaining question at this age.
- New apps work as soon as they are installed, unless they are social or VPN apps.

## Adjust

- When the family is ready, move to **Everything**.
- On school nights, use **No games** for the evening block.
