# Study

For homework hours and exam weeks.

- **Age:** 6 to 16
- **Groups with internet:** 3 of 13
- **Goes well with:** **Exam season**

## Apps

```
[x] System       Phone, messages, keyboard, updates
[x] School       Shad, live class apps
[x] Learning     Schoolwork, languages, books, Quran
[ ] Kids         Digitoon, Aparat Kids, stories
[ ] Messaging    WhatsApp, Eitaa, Bale, iGap
[ ] Daily tools  Maps, taxi, calendar, weather
[ ] Music        Music, radio, podcasts
[ ] Video        Telewebion, Aparat, Filimo
[ ] Games        Every game
[ ] Stores       Bazaar, Myket, Google Play
[ ] Browser      Chrome, Firefox and others
[ ] Social       Rubika, Chatzy, Wisgoon
[ ] Other        Shopping, banking, anything unlisted
```

`[x]` has internet in an Allowed mark. `[ ]` does not. The apps in each group are in [`groups/`](./groups/README.md).

## Why this list

- School apps plus everything for learning: dictionaries, courses, books.
- No messaging, games, video or music, so study time stays study time.
- No browser. It is the easiest way out of studying.

## Adjust

- Older children who research on the web: mark the browser as always in.
- If classmates share homework in a messenger, mark that one app as always in.
