# System

What the phone itself needs. Always has internet in an Allowed mark, in every preset.

## How it is recognised

- **Android system apps:** Every app Android marks as a system app that has no icon of its own
- **Phone, contacts and SMS:** Whichever apps hold these roles on the phone
- **Keyboard:** The active keyboard
- **Pocora:** The child app itself
