## How to read them

- **Package** is the app's Android id. It is how Pocora recognises the app on the child's phone.
- **Ratings on Bazaar** is the number of user ratings on Cafe Bazaar on 8 Mehr 1405. It is a sign of how widely the app is used in Iran, not a score. `-` means the app is not on Bazaar.
- Apps are sorted by that number inside each section.

## How an app gets its group

1. **This list.** An app listed here takes the group it is listed under.
2. **Android's own category.** Any other app takes the category its developer declared: game, video, music, social and so on.
3. **Other.** An app with neither goes to Other.

So the list does not need to hold every app. It needs the apps that matter most and the ones whose own category is wrong or missing.

## Keeping it current

- The list ships with the app and is updated with app releases.
- It holds apps that work on the internet as it is reachable in Iran.
- New entries come from the top lists of Bazaar and Myket.
