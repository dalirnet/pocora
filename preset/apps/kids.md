# Kids

For a young child with a first tablet or a hand-me-down phone.

- **Age:** 8 to 9
- **Groups with internet:** 4 of 13
- **Goes well with:** **One hour a day**, **Weekends only**

## Apps

```
[x] System       Phone, messages, keyboard, updates
[x] School       Shad, live class apps
[x] Learning     Schoolwork, languages, books, Quran
[x] Kids         Digitoon, Aparat Kids, stories
[ ] Messaging    WhatsApp, Eitaa, Bale, iGap
[ ] Daily tools  Maps, taxi, calendar, weather
[ ] Music        Music, radio, podcasts
[ ] Video        Telewebion, Aparat, Filimo
[ ] Games        Every game
[ ] Stores       Bazaar, Myket, Google Play
[ ] Browser      Chrome, Firefox and others
[ ] Social       Rubika, Chatzy, Wisgoon
[ ] Other        Shopping, banking, anything unlisted
```

`[x]` has internet in an Allowed mark. `[ ]` does not. The apps in each group are in [`groups/`](./groups/README.md).

## Why this list

- Only apps made for children, plus school and learning.
- No messaging: at this age a child talks to family on a parent's phone.
- No browser and no store, so nothing new arrives without a parent.
- General games and video are out. Children's video and learning games are in through the Kids group.

## Adjust

- Add one game the child loves as an app that is always in.
- Move to **Everyday** around age 10.
