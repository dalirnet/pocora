# Reachable

For when the child is out: at a class, at a friend's, on the way home.

- **Age:** 6 to 16
- **Groups with internet:** 3 of 13
- **Goes well with:** a custom Allowed block over the hours of an evening class

## Apps

```
[x] System       Phone, messages, keyboard, updates
[ ] School       Shad, live class apps
[ ] Learning     Schoolwork, languages, books, Quran
[ ] Kids         Digitoon, Aparat Kids, stories
[x] Messaging    WhatsApp, Eitaa, Bale, iGap
[x] Daily tools  Maps, taxi, calendar, weather
[ ] Music        Music, radio, podcasts
[ ] Video        Telewebion, Aparat, Filimo
[ ] Games        Every game
[ ] Stores       Bazaar, Myket, Google Play
[ ] Browser      Chrome, Firefox and others
[ ] Social       Rubika, Chatzy, Wisgoon
[ ] Other        Shopping, banking, anything unlisted
```

`[x]` has internet in an Allowed mark. `[ ]` does not. The apps in each group are in [`groups/`](./groups/README.md).

## Why this list

- The child can message the family, see the map and call a taxi.
- Nothing to play or watch, so the phone stays in the pocket.
- The safety uses of a phone keep working when everything else is held back.

## Adjust

- Add the one messenger the family uses if it is not in the Messaging group.
