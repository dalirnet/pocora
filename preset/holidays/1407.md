# 1407

Official holidays of Iran in the year 1407.

- **Holidays:** 25 days, of which 5 fall on a Friday
- **Status:** Calculated ahead. Lunar holidays can move by one day when the official calendar for this year is published.

## Days off in a row

Three or more days with no school, counting Fridays and official holidays.

| From | To | Days | Holidays in it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mon Farvardin 1 | Fri Farvardin 5 | 5 | Nowruz, Martyrdom of Imam Sadiq |
| Fri Khordad 13 | Sun Khordad 15 | 3 | Passing of Imam Khomeini, Tasua, 15 Khordad Uprising, Ashura |
| Fri Tir 31 | Sun Mordad 2 | 3 | Passing of the Prophet, martyrdom of Imam Hasan, Martyrdom of Imam Reza |

## Bridge days

A single ordinary day with a day off on each side. Many families treat it as a day off too.

| Bridge day | Between |
| --- | --- |
| Sat Ordibehesht 24 | Friday and Eid al-Ghadir |
| Thu Mordad 20 | Birthday of the Prophet and Imam Sadiq and Friday |
| Sat Mehr 30 | Friday and Martyrdom of Fatimah |
| Sat Esfand 20 | Friday and Martyrdom of Imam Sadiq |

## Ramadan

Dey 28 to Bahman 26. This is when the **Ramadan** schedule preset fits, outside exam weeks and Nowruz.
