# 1405

Official holidays of Iran in the year 1405.

- **Holidays:** 26 days, of which 3 fall on a Friday
- **Status:** Matches the published calendar for this year.

## Days off in a row

Three or more days with no school, counting Fridays and official holidays.

| From | To | Days | Holidays in it |

| Sat Farvardin 1 | Tue Farvardin 4 | 4 | Nowruz, Eid al-Fitr, Eid al-Fitr, second day |

## Bridge days

A single ordinary day with a day off on each side. Many families treat it as a day off too.

| Bridge day | Between |
| --- | --- |
| Thu Khordad 7 | Eid al-Adha and Friday |
| Sat Shahrivar 7 | Friday and Birthday of the Prophet and Imam Sadiq |
| Thu Dey 3 | Birthday of Imam Ali and Friday |
| Thu Dey 17 | Mabath and Friday |
| Sat Bahman 3 | Friday and Mid-Shaban |
| Sat Esfand 8 | Friday and Martyrdom of Imam Ali |

## Ramadan

Bahman 19 to Esfand 18. This is when the **Ramadan** schedule preset fits, outside exam weeks and Nowruz.
