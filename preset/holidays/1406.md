# 1406

Official holidays of Iran in the year 1406.

- **Holidays:** 27 days, of which 5 fall on a Friday
- **Status:** Calculated ahead. Lunar holidays can move by one day when the official calendar for this year is published.

## Days off in a row

Three or more days with no school, counting Fridays and official holidays.

| From | To | Days | Holidays in it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sun Farvardin 1 | Wed Farvardin 4 | 4 | Nowruz |
| Thu Farvardin 12 | Sat Farvardin 14 | 3 | Islamic Republic Day, Nature Day, Martyrdom of Imam Sadiq |

## Ramadan

Bahman 9 to Esfand 7. This is when the **Ramadan** schedule preset fits, outside exam weeks and Nowruz.
