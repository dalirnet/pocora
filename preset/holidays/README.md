## How the app uses them

- **The list ships with the app** for these five years and is renewed with app releases.
- **The schedule never changes by itself.** A holiday only leads to a suggestion.
- **The day before a holiday,** the parent app suggests giving that day Friday's hours. One tap makes it a change for this week only.
- **No suggestion is made** when the holiday is a Friday, or when the current preset already treats every day alike: Holidays, Summer, Watch only, Nights off and One hour a day.
- **Holidays are marked on the week** in the Schedule tab, so the parent sees them coming.
- **Runs and bridge days** get one suggestion for the whole stretch, not one per day.
- **Nowruz** is covered by the **Holidays** preset, which is suggested for Esfand 25 to Farvardin 13.
- **Ramadan** dates below tell the app when to suggest the **Ramadan** preset.

## How sure the dates are

- **Fixed holidays** are certain.
- **1405** matches the calendar published for the year.
- **1406 to 1409** are calculated ahead. A lunar holiday can move by one day, because the month starts when the new moon is seen. The official calendar for each year is published about a year before it begins, and the list is corrected then.
- **The source** is the time.ir calendar, read on 8 Mehr 1405.
- **Extra closures** announced during the year, for pollution, heat or other reasons, are not holidays and are not in this list.
