# 1409

Official holidays of Iran in the year 1409.

- **Holidays:** 27 days, of which 2 fall on a Friday
- **Status:** Calculated ahead. Lunar holidays can move by one day when the official calendar for this year is published.

Dey 6 to Bahman 4. This is when the **Ramadan** schedule preset fits, outside exam weeks and Nowruz.
