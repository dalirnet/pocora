# 1408

Official holidays of Iran in the year 1408.

- **Holidays:** 28 days, of which 3 fall on a Friday
- **Status:** Calculated ahead. Lunar holidays can move by one day when the official calendar for this year is published.

## Days off in a row

Three or more days with no school, counting Fridays and official holidays.

| From | To | Days | Holidays in it |

| Tue Farvardin 1 | Fri Farvardin 4 | 4 | Nowruz |

| Wed Khordad 3 | Fri Khordad 5 | 3 | Tasua, Ashura |
| Wed Tir 21 | Fri Tir 23 | 3 | Passing of the Prophet, martyrdom of Imam Hasan, Martyrdom of Imam Reza |

## Bridge days

A single ordinary day with a day off on each side. Many families treat it as a day off too.

| Bridge day | Between |
| --- | --- |
| Thu Ordibehesht 7 | Eid al-Adha and Friday |
| Sat Khordad 13 | Friday and Passing of Imam Khomeini |
| Thu Bahman 19 | Eid al-Fitr, second day and Friday |
| Sat Bahman 21 | Friday and Victory of the Revolution |

## Ramadan

Dey 17 to Bahman 16. This is when the **Ramadan** schedule preset fits, outside exam weeks and Nowruz.
