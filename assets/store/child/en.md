# Pocora Child

## Short description

```text
Internet, right on time
```

## Description

```text
Pocora helps families keep their children's internet to the right amount and the right time; parents decide when, how much and with which apps their child goes online. Pocora is about conversation, not control; every report is a reason to talk with your child.

Pocora Child goes on the child's phone and applies the rules the parents set.
It works together with Pocora Parent, installed on the parent's phone.

Features

• Schedule: the hours the internet is on, for each day of the week.
• Apps: which apps have internet in allowed time.
• Data: how much the child may use each hour.
• Alerts: turning Pocora off or installing a VPN app is reported to the parents.
• Openness: the child sees the same rules and alerts as the parents.

Privacy

• No server, no account; the two phones talk only over the home Wi-Fi.
• Messages, photos and websites are never seen.
• Pocora does not bypass the filter and sends nothing off the phone.
• The local VPN is used only to cut internet for apps.

Made for children aged 8 to 16. Free and open source.
```
